Bring your notes to a new device, same privacy posture.

* Restore an encrypted backup onto a fresh device, then unlock with that backup's passphrase.
* Import a backup's notes into your current vault, or replace this device's vault entirely.
* Backups are chosen with the native file picker; a wrong or altered file fails safely.

Still fully offline: no network, no telemetry, no accounts. Not yet audited.
